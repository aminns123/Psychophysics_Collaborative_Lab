# Scientific notes retained during Stage C

Stage C changes authoring structure and the explicitly approved once-only opening.
It does not silently repair or reinterpret an established scientific protocol.
No new mathematical discrepancy was found in the frozen equivalence matrix.

## Pre-existing teaching summary wording

The `SCIENTIFIC_SPEC` dictionaries of `orientation_task`, `probe_above_below` and
`example_experiment` describe their original nonadaptive teaching behaviour.
They predate the selectable adaptive/constant-stimuli teaching methods and still
contain wording such as “None” for adaptation. The selected method's resolved
metadata records its actual method, controlled quantity, schedule/update and
stopping. The dictionaries are frozen and preserved by this migration.

This is a metadata wording limitation, not evidence that the selected method was
ignored. When inspecting a method run, read its selected configuration and
resolved method metadata together with the generic teaching summary. A future
focused change can make the summaries explicitly mode-aware, with provenance and
saved-run compatibility tests. Do not rewrite historical records to update prose.

## Existing scientific interpretation limits

Teaching adaptive tasks are finite demonstrations, not validated threshold
estimators. Constant-stimuli execution does not itself fit a psychometric
function. Incomplete or boundary-affected reversal estimates retain their
existing flags and qualification limits. Gaussian and circular-windowed CSF
remain scientifically distinct envelopes whose thresholds must not be pooled
without justification.

Existing hardware and protocol acquisition safeguards remain in force. Software
equivalence, previews and Test/Ready status do not establish optical contrast,
monitor timing, auditory onset or response latency. Stage C supplies no new
physical qualification evidence.
